# Nicole Vercel App

Build a premium, elegant, modern personal brand website for Nicole Creative Studio, founded by Nicole.



BRAND POSITIONING



Nicole Creative Studio is a creative and executive support brand offering:



- Executive Virtual Assistance

- AI-Powered Executive Support

- Workflow Automation

- Graphic Design

- Social Media Management

- Website Design



The brand should communicate professionalism, innovation, creativity, efficiency, reliability, and sophistication.



DESIGN DIRECTION



Create a luxury, feminine, modern aesthetic using:



- Royal purple

- Soft lavender

- Champagne gold

- White



Use elegant typography, sophisticated layouts, subtle animations, beautiful gradients, premium cards, and generous whitespace.



The website should feel like an international boutique digital agency combined with a high-level executive support consultancy.



HOMEPAGE HERO



Headline:



"Your Vision. My Expertise. Smarter Workflows."



Subheadline:



"I help entrepreneurs, executives, and growing businesses stay organized, streamline operations, and elevate their digital presence through executive support, AI-powered automation, and creative digital solutions."



CTA buttons:



- Work With Me

- Explore My Services



Add a sophisticated animated background and premium visual elements.



ABOUT ME



Headline: "Meet Nicole"



Write a compelling professional introduction positioning Nicole as a proactive Executive Virtual Assistant and creative digital professional who combines administrative excellence, AI tools, automation, and creativity to help businesses operate more efficiently.

- Digital creativity

- Attention to detail

- Organization and reliability
